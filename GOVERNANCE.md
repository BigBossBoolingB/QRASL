# QRASL Governance Model

## 1. Introduction

This document describes the governance model for the QRASL network. The governance model is designed to be decentralized, transparent, and community-driven. All governance activities are managed by the governance module on Shard 6.

## 2. Governance Participants

The governance process involves the following participants:

- **$QRASL Token Holders:** Any holder of $QRASL tokens can participate in governance by creating proposals, seconding proposals, and voting.
- **Delegators:** The 30 public Delegators on Shard 6 have a special role in the governance process. They are responsible for reviewing proposals and providing a recommendation to the community.
- **Validators:** The 30 public Validators on Shard 6 are responsible for implementing the outcome of successful proposals.

## 3. Proposal Process

The proposal process consists of the following stages:

1.  **Proposal Creation:** Any $QRASL token holder can create a proposal by submitting it to the governance module on Shard 6. A proposal must include a detailed description of the proposed change, the rationale behind it, and the expected outcome. A small deposit in $QRASL is required to create a proposal, to prevent spam.

2.  **Seconding:** Once a proposal is created, it must be seconded by a certain number of other token holders. This ensures that there is sufficient interest in the proposal before it moves to the next stage.

3.  **Review by Delegators:** After a proposal has been seconded, it is reviewed by the 30 public Delegators on Shard 6. The Delegators will assess the proposal for its technical feasibility, economic impact, and alignment with the long-term vision of the project. They will then publish a recommendation to the community, which can be "Yes," "No," or "Abstain."

4.  **Voting:** After the Delegators have published their recommendation, the proposal is put to a vote of all $QRASL token holders. The voting period will last for a fixed period of time (e.g., 7 days).

5.  **Implementation:** If a proposal is approved by a majority of the votes, it is then passed to the 30 public Validators on Shard 6 for implementation. The Validators are responsible for ensuring that the proposed changes are implemented correctly and in a timely manner.

## 4. Voting Mechanism

The voting mechanism is based on a one-token, one-vote principle. The weight of a user's vote is proportional to the number of $QRASL tokens they hold. To prevent the concentration of voting power, the governance model may explore the use of quadratic voting or other mechanisms in the future.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Delegators:** The 30 public Delegators are elected by the community and are responsible for providing an initial review of proposals. They are expected to be active members of the community and to have a deep understanding of the QRASL protocol.
- **Validators:** The 30 public Validators on Shard 6 are responsible for the technical implementation of approved proposals. They are expected to have a high level of technical expertise and to be committed to the long-term success of the network.

## 6. Amending the Governance Model

The governance model itself can be amended through the governance process. This allows the community to adapt the governance model to the changing needs of the network.
