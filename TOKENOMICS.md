# QRASL Tokenomics ($QRASL)

## 1. Introduction

This document outlines the tokenomics of the $QRASL token, the native cryptocurrency of the Quantum-Resistant Adaptive Sharded Ledger (QRASL). The tokenomics are designed to ensure the long-term sustainability, security, and decentralization of the network, while also providing incentives for all network participants.

## 2. Token Distribution

The total supply of $QRASL is capped at 10 billion tokens. The distribution is as follows:

| Category                  | Percentage | Amount (in billions) |
| ------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------- |
| **Public Sale**           | 20%        | 2.0                  |
| **Ecosystem Fund**        | 25%        | 2.5                  |
| **Team**                  | 15%        | 1.5                  |
| **Advisors**              | 5%         | 0.5                  |
| **Staking Rewards**       | 30%        | 3.0                  |
| **Reserved for Governance** | 5%         | 0.5                  |
| **Total**                 | **100%**   | **10.0**             |

## 3. Allocation and Vesting

- **Public Sale:** 2 billion tokens will be made available to the public through one or more token sale events.
- **Ecosystem Fund:** 2.5 billion tokens will be allocated to an ecosystem fund to support the growth and development of the QRASL ecosystem. This includes grants for developers, marketing, and community-building initiatives.
- **Team:** 1.5 billion tokens will be allocated to the founding team, subject to a 4-year vesting period with a 1-year cliff.
- **Advisors:** 0.5 billion tokens will be allocated to advisors, subject to a 2-year vesting period.
- **Staking Rewards:** 3 billion tokens will be reserved for staking rewards to incentivize validators and delegators to secure the network.
- **Reserved for Governance:** 0.5 billion tokens are reserved and will be managed by the Shard 6 governance module.

## 4. Burn Mechanisms

To counteract inflation and create long-term value, QRASL will implement several burn mechanisms:

- **Transaction Fees:** A portion of the transaction fees collected on all shards will be burned. The exact percentage will be determined by the governance module on Shard 6.
- **Slashing Penalties:** When validators are slashed for malicious behavior or downtime, a portion of the slashed tokens will be burned.
- **Auction Fees:** Fees from any on-chain auctions (e.g., for scarce resources) will be burned.

## 5. Staking Rewards

Staking rewards will be distributed to validators and delegators who stake their $QRASL tokens. The rewards will be distributed on a per-epoch basis and will be proportional to the amount of stake. The annual inflation rate from staking rewards will be adaptive, targeting a specific staking participation rate to ensure network security.

## 6. Governance

The $QRASL token will also be used for governance. Token holders will be able to vote on proposals related to the development of the network, changes to the protocol, and the allocation of the ecosystem fund. All governance activities will be managed by the governance module on Shard 6.
